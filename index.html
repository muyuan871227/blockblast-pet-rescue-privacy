Privacy Policy

Pet Rescue: Block Puzzle · Effective date: June 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Curry Fish Balls Limited (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, shares, and protects information in connection with the mobile game Pet Rescue: Block Puzzle (the “App”). By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

The App is a single-player block-puzzle game. It does not require you to create an account or sign in, does not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, or camera, and does not offer in-app purchases. Your game progress is stored locally on your device.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to provide personal information directly. The App and our third-party partners automatically collect limited information needed to run the game and to show and measure advertising:

  • Identifiers — device identifiers such as the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), the Identifier for Vendors (IDFV), and similar advertising/device IDs.
  • Device & technical data — device model, operating-system version, language, time zone, and network/connection information.
  • Usage & gameplay data — app open and session times, levels played, progress, scores, settings, and interactions within the App.
  • Advertising data — ad impressions, clicks, and ad-performance/attribution events.
  • Approximate location — a coarse, city/region-level location inferred from your IP address. The App does not request precise (GPS) location.

If you contact us by email for support, we will receive the email address you write from and the contents of your message.

2. How We Use Information

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the App and its features.
  • Save and restore your game progress and preferences on your device.
  • Display advertising and measure ad performance and attribution.
  • Understand how the game is used through aggregated analytics.
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, technical issues, and security risks.
  • Comply with applicable laws and respond to lawful requests.

We do not sell your personal information for money.

4. Advertising & Tracking

The App is supported by advertising. To show and measure ads — including, with your permission, personalized ads — our partners may use your advertising identifier to track activity across other companies’ apps and websites. On first launch we ask for your permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, and you can limit ad personalization in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Apple Advertising. If you decline tracking, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant.

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect the information we handle. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We retain data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above or as required by law, after which it is deleted or anonymized. Game progress stored on your device is kept only on your device and is removed when you delete the App. You can reset your advertising identifier in your device settings at any time.
