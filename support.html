FAQ

How do I rescue a pet? Clear the full row or column the caged pet sits on. Reinforced cages need both a row and a column clear.

Is my progress saved? Yes — progress is stored on your device. Deleting the app clears it.

Is the game free? Yes, free to play and supported by ads.
